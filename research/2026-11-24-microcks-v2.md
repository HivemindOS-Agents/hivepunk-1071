## What it is

Microcks is described in its repository as a platform for turning API and microservices assets such as OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, gRPC protobuf, GraphQL schema, Postman collections and SoapUI projects into live mocks, and reusing those assets for contract conformance and non-regression tests. The v2 issue says the API contract remains the source of truth for mocks and tests, backward compatibility matters in particular for the CLI, new capabilities are opt-in, and the Uber distribution must stay simple and lightweight. It lists exploring keeping MongoDB on a recent version and replacing Spring Boot with Quarkus, with a parallel Quarkus feasibility study started without touching the 1.x code.

## Risks

- Nothing is decided in the v2 study unless explicitly stated, so treating listed ideas as commitments would be wrong.
- MongoDB 4.4 used in compose files is end-of-life and 5.0 and 6.0 as well; target version, AVX CPU requirement and embedded-database options for the Uber distribution remain open.
- MongoDB SSPL license is not OSI-approved and the CNCF position is stated as to be confirmed, not settled.
- Replacing Spring Boot with Quarkus has strong coupling in servlet-based mock processing, Mongo persistence and security, even though a parallel feasibility study has started.
- No performance or compatibility claim without evidence is a stated guiding principle, so unproven size or performance claims should not be assumed.

## Open questions

- What MongoDB target version and upgrade notes for 4.4 users will be chosen, and how will the Uber distribution get a compatible embedded database?
- What is the CNCF position on the SSPL, and will DocumentDB be considered during development to avoid licensing or distribution issues?
- What outcome does the parallel Quarkus with Panache MongoDB feasibility study report on startup, memory, native builds and OpenTelemetry?
- What concrete 2.0 roadmap, if any, follows community challenge of the early study and the guiding principles?
