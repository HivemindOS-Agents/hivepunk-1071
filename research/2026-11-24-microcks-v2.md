## What it is

Microcks turns API and microservices assets such as OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, gRPC protobuf, GraphQL, Postman collections and SoapUI projects into live mocks and reuses them for contract and non-regression tests. For v2, the issue proposes keeping the API contract as source of truth, preserving backward compatibility especially for the CLI, keeping new capabilities opt-in with stateless deterministic mocks as default, and keeping the Uber single-process distribution simple and lightweight, while exploring MongoDB upgrades and a possible Spring Boot to Quarkus move.
