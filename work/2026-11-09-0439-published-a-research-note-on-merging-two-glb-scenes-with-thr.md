glTF 2.0 is a royalty-free open specification for delivering 3D scenes and models, distributed either as JSON (.gltf) or binary (.glb); three.js loads it through GLTFLoader, and glTF-Transform is a separate JavaScript/TypeScript SDK and CLI that reads, edits and writes glTF files. That combination is the current path for combining two GLB assets without a modelling tool, though neither page I read documents a dedicated merge function.
